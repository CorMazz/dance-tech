Privacy Policy

Last updated August 13, 2026

DanceTech stores the minimum we need to run check-in, payments, and advancement exams for Greenville Westies.

What we keep

  • Name, email, and password hash (or Google account id if you sign in with Google).
  • Class roles used to decide which products you can buy.
  • Exam scores, proctor, date, and pass/fail.
  • Shopping cart contents in Redis until they expire or you check out.
  • Payment details on Stripe's side. We do not store card numbers.

Who sees it

You see your own exam history. Proctors and admins see queues, scores, and roles so they can run class. Stripe sees payment data. We do not sell personal information.

How long

Account and exam records stay while you are part of the community, unless you ask us to delete them. Carts expire after about a day.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: corrado@mazzarelli.biz.
