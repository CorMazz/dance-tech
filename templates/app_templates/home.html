Welcome

Hello and welcome! DanceTech is your digital companion for keeping track of progress, grading tests, and enhancing the dance experience for the Greenville Westies community.

Our Purpose

DanceTech was originally built to provide a seamless workflow for community members to navigate our lessons as they progress from beginner+ lessons to advanced levels. It ensures consistency, clarity, and efficiency for both instructors and dancers.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to foster a welcoming and supportive dance community where everyone can grow, learn, and achieve their goals. Through technology, we make grading, tracking, and celebrating achievements simple and enjoyable.
